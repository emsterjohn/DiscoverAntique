PHOTO AND MAP NOTES
- The homepage uses sourced cover images for all 18 municipalities. Some covers are attraction photographs; others are general municipality landscapes where a suitable attraction image was not found.
- Attraction detail pages show only photographs linked to the named attraction. Empty gallery spaces are no longer rendered as fake photo cards.
- Maps are generated as Google Maps search/embed queries from each attraction name and location. Confirm the pin and access point locally before travel.
- Descriptions are introductory summaries based on the linked references. Confirm access, ownership, opening status, facilities, and visitor permissions with the local tourism office.
- Cultural practices, festivals, and archival collections are identified as heritage experiences, not guaranteed daily visitor activities.
- Photographs are loaded from original third-party websites; an internet connection is required. Attribution is not the same as permission. Confirm reuse rights before public/commercial publication.
